CS Resources

CS Resources helps students find what to do with their degree. It brings our career programming, panels and the CS Club Hub together, with more ways to meet companies and learn about CS tracks and courses.

Career programming

We host networking events with companies, including ones that sponsor international students, with group sessions and optional 30-minute one-on-ones with recruiters. Our International Career Panel brings upperclassmen and alumni together to share how they navigated the job market, and Pizza with Professors lets students get to know CS faculty in a relaxed setting.

Panels

Year-round panels on CS tracks and courses, covering requirements, career paths and how to choose, plus advice from alumni. Past events include a Tracks and Degree Panel and an Upperclassmen Panel.

Club Hub

The Club Hub brings together leaders of Purdue's computing clubs to share advice and resources. Each semester it hosts the CS Club Fair, a joint fair where students can find the CS, DS and AI clubs that fit them.
