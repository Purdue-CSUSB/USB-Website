Hello from USB!

The Computer Science Undergraduate Board is dedicated to improving the student experience for CS/DS/AI students at Purdue - whether by managing CS193, hosting forums for student advocacy, or maintaining a database of undergrad-tailored resources, USB is continually creating initiatives that will uphold our values.

About Us

Our Mission

The Computer Science Undergraduate Student Board was established in 1999 to promote a supportive and engaged community within Purdue's Computer Science Department, and to use its relationship with faculty, the Computer Science Corporate Partners Program, and administration to advocate for the student body.

Our Objectives

  • Advocacy

    USB cares about improving the student experience. Visit our daily office hours or attend the undergraduate town hall to discuss ways to improve our community.

  • Events

    We put on events that matter, from fun events like Pizza with Professors and Freshman Social Hour, to professional opportunities through career oriented panels and events.

  • Resources

    We're here to help CS, DS, and AI students. We teach CS193 Tools for first-years, run a free help room for intro CS courses, as well as maintain a tutor list, blog, and student wiki.

      Our Initiatives

      • Blog

        Our written blog posts, spotlights, and video series help in providing a comprehensive understanding of our collective identity and accomplishments.

      • Career Resources

        Provides career guidance and resources for all students, with programming tailored to international students. Includes student panels led by upperclassmen/alumni and a recruiter networking night (round‑robin groups plus optional 1‑on‑1 time).

      • Club Hub

        Club Hub aims to connect the different computing related clubs and organizations within Purdue's CS department and at Purdue as a whole. At the beginning of each semester, this initiative facilitates a CS Club Fair for students to find out about these different organizations and how to get involved.

      • CS193

        In this course organized and taught by our members, we teach essential software engineering tools and more to students beginning their CS/DS/AI journey at Purdue University. Topics include Linux, GitHub, Shell, and more.

      • Help Room

        Our Help Room provides additional tutoring for CS 180, CS 182, and CS 240. This initiative does not intend to and cannot replace regular office hours for these courses, but students in need of extra support may find it here.

      • Mentorship

        This initiative helps incoming freshmen navigate Purdue CS/DS/AI. We pair sophomores—who most recently experienced the first-year journey—with small groups (5–6) of mentees to provide timely, low-commitment, high-impact guidance.

      • Purdue Technical Projects (opens in a new tab)

        USB provides a space for students to collaborate on and promote their Purdue-related technical projects. This initiative connects students with meaningful projects, highlights creativity, and promotes the versatile, interdisciplinary nature of coding.

      • Panels

        Plans and hosts year‑round informational panels (e.g., CS tracks, alumni advice, and more) to offer practical guidance.

      • Research Resources (opens in a new tab)

        Although there are many research opportunities available to students at Purdue, getting started can be difficult or intimidating. This initiative makes the process easier—from reaching out to professors to presenting your work—and provides a consolidated list of projects seeking CS/DS/AI students.

      • Social Wellness

        Focuses on enjoyable, stress‑relieving events that build community and motivation—more “touch‑grass” fun, less direct CS advice.

      • Student Forum/Town Hall

        We bridge communications between the undergraduate student body and the CS/DS/AI faculty by hosting a Student Forum twice a semester to gather student feedback and concerns for the CS department. We also moderate the department town hall once a semester and implement improvements that benefit students.
